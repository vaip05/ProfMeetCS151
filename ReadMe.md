# 🎓 Prof Meet

**Prof Meet** is a scheduling platform designed to simplify how students connect with department professors.  
Built using **Java**, **JavaFX**, and **TypeScript**, the project focuses on clarity, efficiency, and real academic workflows.

The goal of Prof Meet is to eliminate long email chains and replace them with a clear, structured system for booking meetings.

---

# 💡 Inspiration

Scheduling a meeting with a professor should be simple — but often it isn’t.

Unclear office hours, conflicting schedules, and last-minute changes can turn a quick conversation into a frustrating experience.

As students, we wanted a solution that makes professor availability transparent and allows meetings to be scheduled with confidence.

**Prof Meet was created to turn scheduling into a single, reliable process.**

---

# 📌 What It Does

**Prof Meet** allows students to schedule meetings with professors based on **availability and course context**, not just open time.

### Key Features
- View professors by **department**
- Filter availability by **course**
- Schedule meetings within approved time slots
- Prevent double-booking and conflicts
- Allow professors to manage and update availability

The system prioritizes **accuracy and usability**, ensuring meetings are scheduled only when both parties are available.
