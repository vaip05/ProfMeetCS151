#👥 Who We Are

Prof Meet is a scheduling platform built to simplify how students connect with department professors.
The project is developed using Java, JavaFX, and TypeScript, with a focus on accessibility, clarity, and real-world academic workflows.

Prof Meet was created to reduce scheduling friction, eliminate long email threads, and make professor availability transparent and easy to navigate.

💡 Inspiration

As students, we’ve all experienced the frustration of trying to schedule meetings with professors—conflicting office hours, unclear availability, and endless back-and-forth emails.

What should be a quick conversation often turns into a coordination problem.

We envisioned Prof Meet as a centralized solution that allows students to view professor availability by course and department, and schedule meetings confidently within approved time slots.

📌 What It Does

Prof Meet allows users to schedule meetings based on availability and course context, not just open time.

Key Capabilities

View professors by department and course

See real-time available meeting slots

Prevent scheduling conflicts and double bookings

Allow professors to manage and update availability

Support course-specific meetings (e.g., CS101 office hours)

The system prioritizes accuracy and usability, ensuring meetings are scheduled only when both parties are truly available.
